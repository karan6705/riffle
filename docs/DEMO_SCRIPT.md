# Demo video script (≈4 minutes)

Record at 1360×900 for desktop scenes and in the browser's mobile emulation (390×844) for the check. Clear site data first (Me → Reset demo data) so the welcome card shows.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Today** (welcome card) | "Urban streams can turn dangerous within hours, but they're monitored a few times a year. Riffle lets any neighbour check a stream in five minutes, with an AI co-pilot that double-checks *with* them, and it turns every observation into a One Health early warning." |
| 0:20–0:45 | Today: the "Is your stream OK today?" card | "This is the question people actually have. For the Vale das Flores pond, the last check found no animals and the water is warm and green, so Riffle says keep dogs on the lead. The rings are the One Health reading: ecosystem, people, animals." |
| 0:45–1:40 | **Check** on mobile: water → banks → animals | "Plain words and pictures, no jargon. Murky, grey water. On the bug hunt, every group has a 'How to tell' guide. I'll record stoneflies and flat mayflies, which are very sensitive, plus some worms." |
| 1:40–2:30 | **Co-pilot** step | "Only now does the co-pilot reveal what it expected, so it can't bias what I saw. It raises two questions. An expert rule: stoneflies rarely survive sewage. And the model: my animals score higher than a stream that looks like this, and here are the exact observations that drove that. I'm the scientist, so I can go back and check, or confirm with a note: 'two tails, checked with the guide'." |
| 2:30–2:50 | **Result** | "I get a One Health reading with advice, XP for careful science, and a badge. Because a flag is still open, the record is preliminary and goes to an expert. And here is the same check as an HL7 FHIR R4 bundle: Observations, the co-pilot flags as DetectedIssues, and Provenance recording what the citizen, the AI and the expert each did. It validates live." |
| 2:50–3:10 | **Expert review** | "Experts see the citizen's note beside the model's evidence and verify in seconds. We also track how often flags turn out to be valid, which is how we audit the AI." |
| 3:10–3:35 | **Map** → **Site** (Coselhas urban reach) | "For the city, the map shows health or seven-day risk, and what's stressing the streams most. On this reach you can see the decline since construction began, and the presence map shows sensitive insects disappearing quarter by quarter." |
| 3:35–3:55 | **Alerts** (live, then Storm + heatwave test) | "Early warning combines the live Open-Meteo forecast with each site's vulnerability and the latest observations. It gives sewer overflow, heat and bloom, and flash-flood alerts, with concrete actions and notifications." |
| 3:55–4:10 | **Learn** → **Open data** model card | "A storytelling module explains One Health through one raindrop. The model card documents exactly how the AI works and where its limits are. Riffle: from streams to systems." |
